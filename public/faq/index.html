Frequently Asked Questions

Are rates accurate for bank transfers?

No. We show indicative mid-market style rates from free public APIs. Banks and money transfer services add spreads and fees.

Which API do you use?

We call open.er-api.com v6 first. If it fails, we fall back to Fawaz Ahmed currency-api on jsDelivr and pages.dev.

Do you support RWF and CFA francs?

Yes. RWF, CDF, XOF, XAF, KES, UGX, and TZS are included in the default feed and in our SEO landing pages.
