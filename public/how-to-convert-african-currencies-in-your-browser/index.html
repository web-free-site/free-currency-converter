How to convert African currencies in your browser

If you work with francs, shillings, or the Congolese franc, you may want a quick conversion to USD or EUR without creating an account. Free Currency Converter includes these currencies in its default feed and can run in the browser.

Steps

  1. Choose the local currency and the target. Open the converter and select, for example, RWF, XOF, XAF, CDF, KES, UGX, or TZS, then USD or EUR.
  2. Enter the amount and convert. Type the amount you have (or the amount you need) and convert. You can also open a dedicated pair page when you use that pair often.
  3. Read the rate as indicative. The figure comes from a free public API, with an automatic fallback if the first source fails. It is not a bank or mobile-money quote.
  4. Cross-check before you send money. If the conversion matters for a transfer, compare the total you will pay — rate plus fees — with your provider.

CFA francs

West African CFA francs (XOF) and Central African CFA francs (XAF) are different currency codes even though they share a similar history and a peg. Pick the code that matches the country you mean.

Pair pages

Dedicated pages such as RWF to USD or XOF to EUR are shortcuts. They still show indicative rates from the same kind of feed.
